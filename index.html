Privacy comes first

Private is not a public network. Only people with an account can see what happens inside, and you decide what you share.

  • Content is visible to members only
  • Secure sign-in powered by Firebase Authentication
  • Your media is stored on your own Cloudinary account
  • No ads and no public profiles

Common questions

Who can see my posts?

Only members of the community. Posts are not visible to anyone who is not logged in.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Private is designed mobile-first and works in any modern browser.

Is it free to join?

Creating an account is free.

How do I log in again?

Tap Login at the top of this page and sign in with the email and password you used to register.
